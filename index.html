A daily ritual, not a to-do list.

One day at a time, made deliberate.

Moriva is a calm place to plan your day in the morning, focus on what matters, and reflect in the evening. No streaks. No confetti. No nudges that don't earn their place.

Plan → Focus → Reflect

The Loop

Three moments, one day.

In the morning

Plan

Open Moriva and decide what today is for. Add a handful of tasks. Carry forward what's still alive from yesterday — or start fresh. Either is a deliberate choice.

Through the day

Focus

Pick a task. Pick a length. Work. The timer runs on real-world time, not engagement time, and ending early is just ending — not failure.

In the evening

Reflect

A quiet nudge invites you back to write a short journal entry. The day closes the way it opened: on purpose.

Principles

What Moriva refuses to do.

  1. i. No streaks.Missing a day shouldn't feel like losing something.
  2. ii. No celebration sounds.You finished a task. You already know.
  3. iii. No morning notifications.If you opened the app, it's because you wanted to.
  4. iv. No accounts. No servers.Your day stays on your device.

Privacy

Stays on your phone.

Moriva stores your tasks, journal entries, and focus sessions locally on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no third parties. Nothing about your day is transmitted anywhere.
