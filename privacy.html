A note on privacy

What Moriva knows about you.

Moriva is a daily ritual app — plan, focus, reflect. The short version of this page is that we don't collect anything from you. The slightly longer version is below.

Moriva runs entirely on your device. Your tasks, your journal entries, your focus sessions — they stay on your iPhone. They don't come to us, because there is no us on the other end.

What's stored on your device

When you use Moriva, the app keeps a local record of:

  • Daily records One per calendar day, marking when you used the app.
  • Tasks What you planned, completed, or carried over to a new day.
  • Journal entries Anything you write in the evening reflection.
  • Focus sessions Planned and actual duration, and which task you focused on.
  • Your settings Reflection hour, appearance, notification preferences.

All of it is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. It lives in the app's sandbox on your device. Deleting the app removes it. There is also a Reset all data control inside Settings.

What we collect from you

Nothing. Moriva has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising SDKs. The app does not phone home. It does not send your tasks, your journal entries, or any usage data to us or to anyone else.

This isn't a promise to handle your data carefully. It's the absence of a place for your data to go.

System permissions Moriva uses

Notifications

If you turn on the evening reflection reminder, Moriva schedules a local notification at the hour you choose. These notifications are generated and delivered by your device. They don't pass through any server we operate.

Focus & app blocking

If you use focus sessions with app blocking, Moriva uses Apple's Family Controls framework. The list of apps you choose to block is held by the system, not by us — Apple's framework gives Moriva a private, opaque token that it can't read or transmit. We never see which apps you've selected.

Third parties

Moriva does not embed third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, or crash reporting. It does not share data with anyone, because it does not have any data to share.

If, in a future version, Moriva ever adds optional sync, crash reporting, or anything else that involves data leaving your device, this page will change first, and the change will be opt-in.

Children

Moriva is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information about anyone, no information is collected about children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, the date below will change too. Material changes will be noted inside the app before they take effect.
