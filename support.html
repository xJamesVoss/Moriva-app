Support & questions.

Moriva is built to be quiet and stay out of the way. When something goes sideways, here's where to look first.

Common questions

The short answers.

  • Where is my data stored?

    On your device. Moriva does not have servers, accounts, or sync. If you delete the app, your data is gone with it. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

  • Reflection reminders aren't arriving.

    Open Settings → Notifications in Moriva and check that the reflection reminder is enabled. If it's already on, open the system Settings app and confirm Moriva is allowed to send notifications. Restarting your phone resolves most stuck-notification cases.

  • How do I change the reflection time?

    Settings → General → Reflection hour. Changing it reschedules the reminder automatically.

  • Yesterday's unfinished tasks didn't show up today.

    The rollover sheet appears the first time you open Moriva on a new day. If you closed it, the unfinished tasks stay on their original day rather than carrying over. This is intentional — Moriva treats carryover as a deliberate choice, not an automatic one.

  • Can I plan tomorrow in advance?

    Not yet. Right now Moriva only lets you plan today. It's a possible direction for a future version.

  • How do I reset everything?

    Settings → About → Reset all data. This permanently deletes every task, journal entry, and focus session on your device. There is no undo.
